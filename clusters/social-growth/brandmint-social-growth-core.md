## Voice Discipline

All social-growth outputs must follow `voice-and-tone.json`:
- Use preferred vocabulary: requirement, operating logic, user behavior, founder context, systems, signal, coherence, intentional, crafted, shipped, legible, inherited, handoff, delivery loop
- Avoid prohibited terms: consciousness-aligned, disruptive, revolutionary, synergy, best-in-class, full-service agency, etc.
- Maintain the founder-side systems translator voice — peer to peer with the founder, not vendor-to-account

## Content Mix (default for service studio)

| Category | % | Use For |
|----------|---|---------|
| Value (education) | 40% | Explaining delivery loop stages, design principles, founder context |
| Connection (founder story) | 30% | Behind-the-scenes of the studio, how the work happens, who does it |
| Engagement (questions) | 20% | Polls about founder challenges, what they're working on |
| Promotion (CTA) | 10% | Bring the requirement, link to delivery loop |

## Community Engagement Tiers

| Tier | Response Time | Voice | Examples |
|------|---------------|-------|----------|
| Tier 1 (founder inquiry) | < 2 hours | Direct, founder-to-founder | "Bring the requirement..." |
| Tier 2 (operator inquiry) | < 8 hours | Technical, specific | "Here is how the delivery loop runs..." |
| Tier 3 (general follower) | < 24 hours | Restrained, illustrative | "The loop is the studio..." |
| Tier 4 (industry observer) | < 48 hours | Editorial, grounded | "One loop, one team, one handoff..." |

## Review Response Tone

- Acknowledge the observation plainly
- Point to the next concrete step
- Never inflate superlatives
- Use evidence close to the claim
- Maintain calm confidence — not celebration theater
