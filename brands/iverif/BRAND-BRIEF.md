# iverif brand brief

iverif remains the brand name. Public descriptor: **AI document validation for
energy subsidy operators**. Brand site: **iverif.fr**. Wiki / product surface:
**iverif.io**. Primary GTM language and region: **fr-FR / France**.

The product helps energy subsidy operators validate dossiers against
programme-specific rules before submission. France GTM centres on **CEE** and
**Primes Énergie**, with programme coverage also spanning Conto Termico, BEG,
ECO4, and MOVES III.

## Positioning spine (from inputs/v1)

- Category: RegTech SaaS for EU energy subsidy document validation.
- Core problem: avoidable document errors that put subsidy claims at risk.
- Difference: pre-configured programme rules plus managed onboarding — not
  generic OCR or storage-only DMS.
- Voice: precise, authoritative, quietly confident. No hype.
- Aspirational craft references: Stripe, Notion, Vercel, Linear.

## Primary audience

**Marie Durand** — Operations Manager at a mid-sized French energy services /
délégataire organisation (≈50–500 employees). She runs CEE dossier back-office
work and must keep obligé vs délégataire roles clear when talking to the
market. Decision criteria: accuracy, time-to-value, auditor trust, and
integration without heavy IT projects.
