# Voice and tone

## Attributes

**Precise · authoritative · quietly confident · no-hype.**

Speak as the trusted CEE compliance expert — senior colleague, not a hype vendor. Relationship: technical guide for back-office and compliance.

## EN register

Clear B2B RegTech English; keep regulatory terms accurate; active voice; no superlatives. Prefer short sentences that name the programme early when FR market context applies.

## FR register (when writing French)

- Vouvoiement by default; tutoiement banned in FR commercial copy
- Preferred terms: dossier, pièce justificative, fiche d'opération standardisée, obligé, délégataire, prime énergie, certificats d'économies d'énergie, contrôle avant dépôt, piste d'audit
- Avoid calques: game-changer, révolutionnaire, seamless, next-gen, AI magic, disruptif

## Good / bad

| Good | Bad |
| --- | --- |
| iverif checks documentary coherence of your CEE dossiers before submission and keeps a timestamped audit trail. | iverif revolutionises energy compliance with magical AI that guarantees zero rejections. |

Stripe, Notion, Vercel, Linear are **aspirational craft** references — never partnerships (ledger F4).
