# Product overview

## Category

RegTech SaaS for **EU energy subsidy document validation**. Public descriptor: **AI document validation for energy subsidy operators**.

## Problem

Operators must cross-check many supporting documents per dossier against programme-specific rules. Manual validation is slow, error-prone, hard to audit, and brittle when volumes spike. Avoidable documentation errors put subsidy claims at risk.

## Solution (bounded)

iverif is an AI validation platform that:

- Checks dossiers against **programme-specific rules** before submission
- Emphasises **cross-document consistency** (not extraction alone)
- Ships with **managed programme configuration / onboarding**
- Keeps **timestamped audit logs** for regulatory scrutiny

France GTM centres on **CEE** and **Primes Énergie**. Programme coverage intent also lists Conto Termico, BEG, ECO4, and MOVES III (ledger F2). Depth per programme is **not** independently audited in the current ledger — do not over-promise.

## Positioning (EN)

For operations and compliance leads at mid-market French CEE délégataires / operators, iverif is a RegTech AI document-validation platform that checks dossiers against programme rules before submission — reducing avoidable errors and preserving an audit trail — unlike generic OCR, storage-only DMS, or primes-operator brands (Hellio, Effy, and peers) that run the market without shipping this validation software.

## Not this category

- Primes marketplace / renovation consumer brand
- Generic IDP / OCR-only tool
- Obligation-scheme operator (obligé or délégataire)
- Government or PNCEE affiliate

## Proof boundary

Internal aspirations about rejection rates, seconds-per-dossier, or go-live SLAs remain **blocked** until dated iverif receipts exist. See [claims](./claims.md).
