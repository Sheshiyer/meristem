# Press guidance

## Targets

- **Les Échos** — FR business newswire for product / milestone announce
- **JDN** — RegTech / SaaS thought and product announce
- EN mirrors for international desks

## Angle

AI document validation for energy subsidy operators; France GTM on CEE / Primes Énergie; software that helps back-offices catch avoidable errors before submission.

## Hard no

- Invented funding figures, customer counts, or accuracy SLAs
- PNCEE partnership claims
- Competitor euro amounts
- “Revolutionary AI” hype

Use factual tone; dateline/city per press-release spoke FR conventions when publishing French.
