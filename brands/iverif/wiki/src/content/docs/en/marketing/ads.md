# Ads guidance

## Primary surface

**LinkedIn FR** for Marie Durand titles (Responsable Back-Office CEE, Chef de projet CEE, Compliance CEE, Ops délégataire/obligé). EN variants support EU desks / investors — not the primary paid surface.

## Creative rules

- Lead with dossier pain + pre-submission validation
- Name CEE / Primes Énergie early in FR creatives
- Separate software validation from primes-operator brands
- Explee drafts are **draft-only — no POST** from Meristem

## Sample angle (EN)

“Your team still finds document errors after deposit. iverif checks CEE dossier coherence before submission — with an audit trail you can explain.”

## Do not

Guarantee outcomes, cite blocked metrics, imply PNCEE endorsement, or run competitor-bashing with unverified feature matrices.
