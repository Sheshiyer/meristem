## BLOCKED until proved

- Rejection-rate reduction / “30 seconds” / “go-live 5–7 days”
- Obligé / délégataire / government affiliation
- Competitor euro amounts / “jusqu’à X%”
- Market-size figures without dated primary source
- PNCEE partnership
- Unaudited GDPR / EU-hosting assurances

Supported facts and observations: see ledger + canonical claims pages.
