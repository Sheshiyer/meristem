# iverif Messaging Direction Summary

## The one-line position

**iverif is AI document validation for energy subsidy operators** — France GTM leads with **CEE / Primes Énergie** pre-submission dossier checks, programme rules, and audit trails.

FR twin: **Validation documentaire AI pour opérateurs de subventions énergétiques** (CEE / Primes Énergie en premier).

## The problem worth solving

Energy subsidy dossiers require cross-checking many supporting documents against programme-specific rules. Manual validation is slow, error-prone, hard to audit, and does not scale when volumes spike. Avoidable documentation errors put subsidy claims at risk.

## The product promise

Give operators an explainable **pre-submission validation signal**: programme-configured checks, cross-document consistency, and timestamped audit logs — with managed onboarding, not DIY rule engineering.

## Why this is different

1. **Validation software, not primes distribution.** Hellio, Effy, and peer operators run market journeys; iverif checks dossiers before deposit.
2. **Programme rules, not generic OCR.** Extraction alone is not enough; coherence against fiches / programme criteria is the job.
3. **Audit trails for scrutiny.** Operators need what was checked, when, and against which rule shape — without inventing affiliation with PNCEE or government bodies.
4. **FR-first, multi-programme intent.** Public FR GTM centres on CEE / Primes Énergie; programme list also includes Conto Termico, BEG, ECO4, MOVES III as scope intent (ledger F2) without over-claiming depth per programme.

## Audience and distribution

Primary ICP: **Marie Durand**, Operations Manager in a mid-market French délégataire / energy-services back-office (≈50–500 employees). Keep **obligé** vs **délégataire** language accurate (ledger P5).

Primary channels (research/channel-plan.md): LinkedIn FR, Welcome to the Jungle, JDN, PNCEE (context only), Les Échos. Tag all campaign orchestration **FR GTM**. Domains: brand **iverif.fr**, wiki **iverif.io**.

## Voice

Precise, authoritative, quietly confident. No hype. FR commercial copy uses vouvoiement and exact CEE vocabulary. Aspirational craft refs (Stripe, Notion, Vercel, Linear) are not partnerships (F4).

## Non-negotiable proof rules

- Do **not** publish rejection-rate reductions, “30 seconds”, “go-live 5–7 days”, or market-size figures until dated iverif receipts exist (ledger prohibited).
- Do **not** claim iverif is an obligé, délégataire, or government-affiliated body.
- Do **not** reuse competitor euro primes amounts or “jusqu’à X%” language.
- Treat PNCEE as institutional context — never as endorsement.
- Do not POST Explee / ad-platform drafts from Meristem spokes.
