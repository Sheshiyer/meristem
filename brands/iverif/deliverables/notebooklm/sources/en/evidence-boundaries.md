# Evidence boundaries

Public-source facts cover the CEE scheme, standardised fiches, and obligé/délégataire roles. First-party facts cover brand naming, programme scope intent, assets, and voice lock. Prohibited: rejection-rate guarantees, affiliation claims, competitor euro amounts, unaudited market-size figures, PNCEE endorsement.
