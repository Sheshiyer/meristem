### Pain Points

- Manual cross-checking of 10-20 documents per dossier is time-consuming and error-prone
- Rejection only discovered after submission, when correction is expensive
- Each EU programme has unique rules that change frequently
- Maintaining compliance across multiple subsidy programmes is a full-time job
- No audit trail for validation checks when regulators question dossiers

### Goals

- Reduce dossier rejection rate to under 2%
- Validate full dossiers in under 30 seconds
- Maintain 100% audit-trail coverage for regulatory compliance
- Onboard new programmes in days, not months
- Scale submission volume without proportional team growth

### Objections

- Current manual process works 'well enough'
- Concern about AI accuracy with regulatory documents
- Integration complexity with existing CRM/document systems
- Cost vs. current headcount allocation

## Competitor Analysis

### Market Gaps

- No existing tool built specifically for EU energy subsidy document validation
- Generic OCR tools cannot handle cross-document consistency checking
- Legacy document management lacks programme-specific rule engines
- Self-serve SaaS requires operators to configure complex validation rules alone
- Single-market tools (e.g., French CEE-only) cannot serve multi-country operators

### Technical Benchmark


#### Dimensions

- **Processing Speed:** 30 seconds per full dossier
- **Document Types:** Unlimited — invoices, attestations, certificates, technical sheets, affidavits
- **Programme Coverage:** 6 pre-configured EU programmes + custom configurations
- **Accuracy Target:** <2% rejection rate

- **Materials:**
  - Generative AI for structured data extraction
  - Cross-document reasoning engine
  - Configurable rule engine per programme
  - EU-hosted infrastructure (GDPR compliant)
  - Full API for CRM/integration
