## Buyer Persona

### Meta Project

- **Product Name:** iverif.io Platform
- **Persona Name:** The Compliance Operator
- **Tone Source:** Precise, authoritative, quietly confident. Technical depth delivered with clarity. No hype — just proven results.

### Voice Samples

- **Professional:** Cross-check every field across 10-20 documents per dossier in under 30 seconds.
- **Authoritative:** Pre-configured for CEE, Conto Termico, BEG, ECO4, Primes Énergie, and MOVES III.
- **Confident:** <2% rejection rate target. 100% audit-trail coverage. GDPR compliant — EU-hosted.

### Emotional Drivers

- **Primary:** Regulatory peace of mind — never lose a subsidy claim to avoidable errors again
- **Secondary:** Operational pride — validate dossiers with the precision their work deserves
- **Tertiary:** Time reclaimed — hours of manual cross-checking reduced to seconds

### Demographics

- **Job Titles:**
  - Back-office Manager
  - Compliance Officer
  - Dossier Processor
  - Subsidy Coordinator
  - Operations Director
- **Company Size:** 10-500 employees
- **Company Type:** Energy subsidy operators, ESCos, aggregators, retrofit coordinators

### Pain Points

- Manual cross-checking of 10-20 documents per dossier is time-consuming and error-prone
- Rejection only discovered after submission, when correction is expensive
- Each EU programme has unique rules that change frequently
- Maintaining compliance across multiple subsidy programmes is a full-time job
- No audit trail for validation checks when regulators question dossiers

### Goals

- Reduce dossier rejection rate to under 2%
- Validate full dossiers in under 30 seconds
- Maintain 100% audit-trail coverage for regulatory compliance
- Onboard new programmes in days, not months
- Scale submission volume without proportional team growth

### Objections

- Current manual process works 'well enough'
- Concern about AI accuracy with regulatory documents
- Integration complexity with existing CRM/document systems
- Cost vs. current headcount allocation
