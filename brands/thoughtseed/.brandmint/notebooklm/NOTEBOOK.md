## Notebook Focus

This notebook is **about the flow** — the delivery loop, the stages, the inheritance, the voice discipline. It is **not** about:

- ICP demographics (Mira is named once in the source pack, but only as a founder who carries the requirement — not as a market segment to analyze)
- Product features or industry verticals (the loop runs across web, mobile, AI, automation, connected products — none of those are foregrounded)
- Pricing, timelines, or competitive positioning
- Internal methodology named in public copy

When you ask the notebook questions, it will speak about **what the loop does and how it sounds**, not about **what the loop sells and to whom**.
