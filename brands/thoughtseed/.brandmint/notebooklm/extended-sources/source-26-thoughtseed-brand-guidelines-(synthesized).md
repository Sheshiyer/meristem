### Item 4

- **Section Number:** 4

- **Title:** Voice & Tone

- **Content:** Voice matrix: formality 0.55, enthusiasm 0.4, technicality 0.6, warmth 0.5, authority 0.7. Tone variations: announcement (calm confidence, state what shipped), support (steady, exact, point to next step), education (explicit, structured, low-drama), crisis (serious, grounded, evidence-close), sales (confident, concrete, scoped not pressured). Channel calibration: website hero (spare, atmospheric), proposal (named requirement first), technical docs (low-drama, inheritable), case study (evidence-led), founder note (peer-to-peer).
