## Rationale

### Brand Attributes

- disciplined

- foundational

- editorially restrained

- tactile

- growth-oriented

- **Color Psychology:** Deep Quantum Blue anchors the studio's long-horizon trust and discipline. Teal serves as the directional signal — clear, calm, technically precise. Energetic Orange ignites action without screaming. Mindful Gray scaffolds without distracting. Growth Light signals emergence without falling into wellness/consciousness framing.

- **Competitor Differentiation:** Explicitly avoids generic SaaS purple (forbidden). The palette is editorial-documentary, not corporate-bright or wellness-pastel. It's tuned for restraint and craft, not for conversion-optimization brightness.

## Usage Guidelines

### Primary

#### Do

- Use as anchor background for editorial contexts

- Pair with white or Growth Light for high-contrast typography

- Apply to dark metal emblem and triptych left panel

- Use for hero backgrounds where long-horizon trust is needed

#### Dont

- Don't apply purple-tinted variations (#7C3AED is forbidden)

- Don't pair with gradients that look SaaS-template

- Don't use at low contrast against white (#3F51B5 fails AA)

### Secondary

#### Do

- Use as directional signal in editorial compositions

- Apply to metallic 3D object surfaces and triptych center/right

- Pair with Deep Quantum Blue for cool editorial contrast

- Use for glass logo and accent surfaces

#### Dont

- Don't use at low opacity against white (contrast drops below AA)

- Don't pair with stock wellness pastels (forbidden framing)
