## Logo Type

- **Primary:** wordmark + tree mark (icon)

- **Rationale:** The Thoughtseed horizontal wordmark carries primary brand identity. The tree mark (circular canopy with structured trunk and root system) is the icon variant. The wordmark reads first; the tree mark carries the brand at small sizes and in monochromatic contexts. Both are codified from the original logo references and never regenerated. Generated logo-object imagery (dark metal emblem, metallic 3D, glass, wax seal, concrete-botanical installation) are art-direction references and material expressions of the canonical mark, never replacement logos.

## Style Direction

- **Complexity:** simple — the canonical mark is minimal and structural; generated logo objects add material complexity as art direction

- **Weight:** medium — the tree mark carries visible weight without becoming heavy

- **Shape:** geometric-organic — circular canopy (geometric) with structured trunk and root system (organic-mechanical)

- **Style:** modern with editorial restraint

- **Mood:** serious, crafted, grounded

## Usage Specs

- **Minimum Size:** Wordmark: 80px wide. Tree mark: 24px height. Logo objects: 320px minimum for material detail to read.

- **Clear Space:** Minimum clear space = 1× the height of the tree mark, on all sides. For wordmark: 1× the cap-height of the wordmark.

### Dont Do

- Do not regenerate the canonical wordmark or tree mark. They are codified.

- Do not use logo objects as replacements for the canonical mark in formal contexts (decks, contracts, press kits).

- Do not stretch, skew, or recolor the canonical mark outside the approved palette.

- Do not apply the mark at low contrast (<4.5:1) on backgrounds.

- Do not place logo objects inside purple SaaS gradients.

- Do not bake text into logo object renders — the canonical typography is always rendered separately.
