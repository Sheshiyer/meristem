## Visual Principles

### Item 1

- **Name:** Digital Wilderness

- **Description:** Disciplined engineering meets organic emergence. Architectural precision against cultivated texture. The brand sits at the seam where rigor and growth meet — neither clinical nor lush, but held together.

### Item 2

- **Name:** Editorial-documentary photography

- **Description:** Real prototyping spaces, not staged studios. Tactile materials. Working environments. Evidence sits next to every claim. Photography looks like feature stories in a design or technology publication, not startup marketing.

### Item 3

- **Name:** Material honesty

- **Description:** Surfaces read as themselves: matte paper has grain, brushed aluminum has direction, weathered stone has texture, living moss is alive. No glossy plastic, no stock gradients, no fake texture. The materials carry the meaning.

### Item 4

- **Name:** Restraint over excess

- **Description:** Generous negative space, limited labels, clear modular grids. The brand speaks quietly. Atmosphere comes from phrasing and image choice, not volume. Premium restraint, not loud luxury.

### Item 5

- **Name:** Bento-grid composition

- **Description:** Editorial layouts use a 6-module bento structure: hero, social, palette, typography specimen, logo block, brand DNA. Each module earns its place. The grid is structural, not decorative.

### Item 6

- **Name:** Material expressions of the logo

- **Description:** The tree mark translates across premium materials — dark metal, polished chrome, embossed glass, wax seal, holo-vinyl, concrete-botanical installation — without ever being replaced. Material variations are art-direction references, not replacement marks.

## Photography

### Style

- **Lighting:** Soft architectural daylight with dramatic single-source side lighting. Screen glow accents from active displays. Warm Energetic Orange highlights on prototype surfaces.

- **Composition:** Rule of thirds with text-safe zones for hero/lifestyle. Editorial framing. Knolling for product shots. Wide cinematic for establishing shots.

- **Color Treatment:** Branded tint — Deep Quantum Blue (#1A237E) shadows, Teal (#00897B) accent tones, Mindful Gray (#37474F) neutrals. Energetic Orange (#F57C00) ignition. Growth Light (#B8E986) for organic signals.

- **Subject Focus:** Founders and operators in prototyping spaces — not stock photo models. System artifacts as the subject matter: screens showing delivery dashboards, hand-drawn requirement sketches on matte paper, working prototypes on brushed aluminum.

- **Mood:** Premium, sophisticated, real. The feeling of a serious design studio that cares about craft. Working environments, not staged sets.

- **Context:** Editorial-documentary. The kind of imagery that appears in design publications (Fast Company, Wired, Awwwards) as feature stories, not marketing pages.

### References

- Editorial feature photography from Fast Company and Wired founder profiles

- Architecture photography from Brutalist and Contemporary museum installations

- Studio documentation photography from Pentagram and IDEO project archives

- Industrial design photography from Dezeen and Wallpaper*

## Illustration

### Style

- **Type:** geometric systems diagrams blended with organic signal patterns

- **Complexity:** medium — diagrams are legible, not overworked

- **Line Weight:** consistent, measured — technical drawing aesthetic

- **Color Approach:** primary palette with restrained accents — no rainbow gradients

- **Character Style:** no characters; pure diagrammatic illustration

- **Prompt Template:** Geometric systems diagram for Thoughtseed — a founder-led systems studio. [Subject: e.g., delivery loop, requirement architecture, handoff system]. ## Guidelines

### Do

- Use editorial-documentary photography for hero, lifestyle, and product shots

- Anchor compositions with Deep Quantum Blue shadows and Teal accents

- Apply bento-grid structure to brand overview and identity compositions

- Translate the tree mark across premium materials as art direction

- Use generous negative space and restrained typography

- Pair every claim with the artifact that proves it

- Show real prototyping spaces with working artifacts

- Maintain material honesty — surfaces read as themselves

### Dont

- Don't use purple SaaS gradients

- Don't use stock startup photos

- Don't use empty futurism (chrome, glow, sterile surfaces)

- Don't use mystical fog or mystical atmosphere

- Don't use glossy plastic textures

- Don't use cartoon icons

- Don't bake text into hero imagery — render typography separately

- Don't name the internal methodology or its metaphors in public copy

- Don't use 'conscious/consciousness' framing in public copy

- Don't use 'full-service agency' framing — the studio is scoped, scoped, scoped
