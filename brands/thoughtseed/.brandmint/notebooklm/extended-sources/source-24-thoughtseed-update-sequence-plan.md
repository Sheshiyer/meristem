# Source 24: Thoughtseed Update Sequence Plan

*Campaign update types — kickoff, mid-engagement, milestone, handoff. Email + LinkedIn + X coordination.*

_Source: meristem pipeline output at `wave-6b-social-growth/update-strategy-sequencer.json`_

---

## Update Types

### Item 1

- **Type:** kickoff

- **When:** Day 0 of engagement

- **Voice:** Calm confidence, state what begins

- **Frequency:** Once per engagement

### Item 2

- **Type:** mid_engagement

- **When:** Day 7-14 of engagement

- **Voice:** Steady, exact, point to next step

- **Frequency:** Every 1-2 weeks during active engagement

### Item 3

- **Type:** milestone_reached

- **When:** When requirement is locked, system is designed, build ships, handoff completes

- **Voice:** Reflective, evidence-led

- **Frequency:** Per major milestone (3-4 per engagement)

### Item 4

- **Type:** handoff_announcement

- **When:** Day of ownership transfer

- **Voice:** Quietly proud, peer to the founder

- **Frequency:** Once per engagement, at end

## Sequences

### Item 1

- **Id:** kickoff-001

- **Update Type:** kickoff

#### Subject Lines

- We have the requirement. Here is what happens next.

- Your build is on the calendar

- From requirement to handoff: where we start

- **Body:** Thanks for bringing the requirement to us. The short version of what you've signed up for: one team carries framing, behavior, product, engineering, and handoff inside a single delivery loop. What happens next: 1. We confirm the kickoff time and who joins from your side. 2. We open with the requirement: the problem, the people who will use the system, and the way you want to run it. 3. From there we design, build, and hand it back in a form your team can own. You will always talk to the operators doing the work, not an account layer.

#### Channels

- email

- **Cta:** Confirm your kickoff details

- **Scheduled For:** Immediate upon kickoff

### Item 2

- **Id:** mid-001

- **Update Type:** mid_engagement

#### Subject Lines

- Where the loop is right now

- Quick note on where we are

- **Body:** Quick note on where the loop is right now: [stage name]. Here is what we have, what we're doing next, and what we need from you. [Specific progress items]. [Specific next step]. Nothing is at risk. If you have questions, reply to this email.

#### Channels

- email

- **Cta:** Reply to this email

- **Scheduled For:** Day 7-14

### Item 3

- **Id:** milestone-001-requirement-locked

- **Update Type:** milestone_reached

#### Subject Lines

- The requirement is locked

- We're moving to system design

- **Body:** The requirement is locked. Here is the framing we landed on: [brief description of the locked requirement]. The next stage is system design — interface and operating logic together, with user behavior treated as part of the system. From here: [timeline]. If anything in the locked requirement needs adjustment, this is the right moment to flag it.

#### Channels

- email

- linkedin

- **Cta:** Review the locked requirement

- **Scheduled For:** When requirement stage completes

### Item 4

- **Id:** milestone-002-build-shipped

- **Update Type:** milestone_reached

#### Subject Lines

- The system shipped to production

- We are at handoff

- **Body:** The system shipped to production. What was framed is what we built — the loop held. Next stage is handoff: documentation, training, control surfaces, the Ownership Transfer Pack. From here: [handoff timeline]. You inherit a system you can run, extend, and own without us.

#### Channels

- email

- linkedin

- **Cta:** Schedule handoff session

- **Scheduled For:** When build stage completes

### Item 5

- **Id:** handoff-001

- **Update Type:** handoff_announcement

#### Subject Lines

- The system is yours to run

- Ownership transfer complete

- What you inherit

- **Body:** The system is yours to run. Here is what you inherit: a running system, not a deck disconnected from shipped reality. Documentation written to be read by the team that will run it. Control surfaces and operational artifacts that keep the system legible. A model of the problem you can carry forward without us. We are not an always-on retainer. If a new entangled requirement appears, you can come back for a new engagement.

#### Channels

- email

- linkedin

- x

- **Cta:** Reach out when you need us

- **Scheduled For:** Day of handoff

- **Voice Compliance:** PASS — all sequences use preferred vocabulary, no prohibited terms, founder-side systems translator voice
