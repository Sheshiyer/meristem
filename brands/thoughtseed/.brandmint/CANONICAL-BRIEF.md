## One-liner
Thoughtseed is a **founder-led systems studio** that turns complex requirements into
coherent technology systems — from requirement to handoff — inside one delivery loop.

## Positioning statement
Thoughtseed is a founder-led systems studio that turns complex requirements into coherent
products by aligning user psychology, founder operating logic, interface design, engineering
delivery, and structured handoff.

## Brand promise
One coherent system from requirement to handoff.

## Messaging hierarchy (use verbatim where copy is needed)
- **Headline:** Digital Wilderness
- **Tagline:** We plant ideas. They grow wild.
- **Subhead:** Thoughtseed turns complex requirements into coherent systems by aligning user
  psychology, founder operating logic, interface design, and technical delivery.
- **Body:** A founder-led systems studio for problems that cannot be split across disconnected

## Hero offer & service modules
- **Hero product:** Thoughtseed Studio.
- **Requirement Architecture:** problem framing, psychology mapping, operating constraints,
  decision logic before implementation.
- **Product & Interface Systems:** web, mobile, AI, automation, connected-product execution
  with behavior treated as part of the system.
- **Handoff & Decision Systems:** documentation, training, control surfaces, operational
  artifacts that make the delivered work inheritable.
- **Engagement models:** System Sprint (short framing/prototype cycle); Requirement-to-Handoff
  Build (scoped delivery from framing through ownership transfer).

## Audience — primary persona
- **Name:** The System-Carrying Founder ("Mira"). Founder / CTO / innovation lead / product
  strategist with a lean senior team.
- **Wants:** solve an important problem without becoming the full-time vendor translator; one
  studio that understands requirement + user + operating reality; a system they can run after delivery.
- **Frustrations:** single-slice vendors; decks disconnected from shipped reality; engineering that
  ignores adoption/trust; shallow AI proposals; systems that break at ownership transfer.
- **Worldview:** skeptical of buzzwords, attracted to integrated thinking, pays for competence when
  trust is earned, prefers ownership over dependency.
- **Respects:** founder memos, case studies, handoff notes, structured decks, technical walkthroughs,
  system diagrams. **Notices:** specificity, coherent language, taste with restraint, evidence of shipping.

## Competitive frame
Category: founder-led systems studio for requirement-to-handoff delivery. Differentiators:
one integrated studio logic; founder-led judgment (less translation loss); psychology-aware
framing grounded in build reality; handoff as part of the system; studio-plus-IP (reusable
product IP, not pure services); a distinct visual/verbal territory beyond agency sameness.

## Voice & tone
- **Persona:** the founder-side systems translator — operators who have built systems and lived
  with ambiguity. Clear, calm, technically precise, human, founder-led, editorially restrained.
- **Preferred language:** requirement, operating logic, user behavior, founder context, systems,
  signal, coherence, intentional, crafted, shipped, legible, inherited, handoff, cross-disciplinary,
  delivery loop.
- **AVOIDED language (never in public copy):** consciousness-aligned, conscious growth, consciousness
  technology, organic growth operations, disruptive, revolutionary, synergy, best-in-class,
  future-proof, cutting-edge, guru, healing journey, vibration, AMC, full-service agency.
- **Writing rules:** short declarative sentences; evidence close to the claim; ambitious ideas kept
  calm; atmosphere from phrasing and image choice, not abstraction; write like someone who ships;
  describe downstream effects of the process, not internal mechanics; not an always-on retainer agency.

## LAYER DISCIPLINE (critical anti-leak rule)
- **Backstage / internal only:** the Krebs Cycle of Creativity, requirement-to-handoff loop internals,
  consciousness/bio-hacking research origins. NEVER the public headline.
- **Frontstage / public:** the visible EFFECTS — clearer requirements, calmer execution, stronger
  trust and adoption, cleaner ownership transfer, more coherent systems.
- If a spoke produces public-facing copy and it contains any avoided term or names the Krebs cycle /
  consciousness as a category claim → it is WRONG. Translate it into effects instead.

- **Display:** Tyros Pro — hero lines, section thresholds, ceremonial headings.
- **Body/editorial:** SubjectivitySerif — narrative text, case-study copy.
- **Data/ops:** Fira Code — technical labels, metadata, diagrams, section numbering.
