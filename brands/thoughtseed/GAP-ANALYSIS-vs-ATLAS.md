## ⚠️ CONFLICTS — founder decision (do NOT auto-merge)

1. **Voice / layer-discipline leak (most important).** The live atlas surfaces the *backstage* layer
   publicly, which the refined `voice-and-tone` explicitly bans:
   - Studio Model differentiator: **"Conscious technology framing without vague wellness language"**
   - Audience objection: **"'Consciousness-aligned sounds vague' → ground it in shipped systems"**
   - Research report title: **"…Comprehensive Study Guide to the Conscious Systems Studio"**
   The mint's refined brand translates these to *effects* and keeps "conscious/consciousness" out of
   public copy. → **Recommend scrubbing the "conscious/consciousness" framing from the public atlas.**
2. **Service model divergence.**
   - Atlas: domains **Brand / Product / Growth Systems**; engagement **"Studio Partner" (ongoing
     founder-to-founder)**.
   - Mint: modules **Requirement Architecture / Product & Interface Systems / Handoff & Decision
     Systems**; engagement **"Requirement-to-Handoff Build" (scoped, handoff-first, explicitly NOT a
     retainer)**.
   These are two different studio shapes (ongoing partner vs scoped requirement-to-handoff). → Founder
   call on which is canonical; everything downstream should follow it.
3. **Wording (minor):** atlas "founder-led **technology** studio" vs mint "founder-led **systems**
   studio"; persona "The Cross-Disciplinary Builder" vs "The System-Carrying Founder" (both "Mira").
