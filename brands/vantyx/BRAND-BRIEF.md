## The name
**Vantyx** — coined from **vantage point**, the one thing a view-led buyer actually pays for. The `-yx` makes it ownable, premium, and platform-scalable (not tied to one project). It promises *the best seat in the building, on demand.*

## Essence
**Living vantage** — the view, kept alive and true as the building rises.

## Mission
> Vantyx exists to help developers **sell the view** — by turning every floor's real, ever-changing outlook into an immersive experience buyers can step into, and keep true as the building rises.

## Vision
> A world where no one buys a home sight-unseen — where the view from any floor, at any hour, is one tap away and always true to today.

1. **The view is the product** — everything serves the fidelity of what buyers will see.
2. **Never go dark** — a live sales tool can't break; updates are non-destructive + atomic.
3. **Builders, not engineers** — marketing teams update it; if it needs a manual, we failed.
4. **Always true to today** — a two-year-old view is a lie; current is the whole point.

## Story (origin)
We built a one-off 360 tour for a real tower (Marina One, Bengaluru) — beautiful, but the moment construction moved, the views were wrong, and fixing them meant a rebuild every time. Keeping them current was destructive and risky, so it just didn't happen. So we built the fix: re-capture and publish floor views yourself, non-destructively, with the live tour never going dark. **Vantyx keeps the view as true as the building it sells.**
