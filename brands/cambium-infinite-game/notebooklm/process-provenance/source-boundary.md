# NotebookLM Source Boundary

## Included remotely

Only the authored product documents in `notebooklm/product-sources/` are eligible for upload:

1. product overview;
2. operating model;
3. positioning and language;
4. visual system;
5. product brief and proof status.

## Excluded remotely

- `.brandmint/prompts/`;
- `.brandmint/outputs/`;
- runner logs and state files;
- market-research scratch notes;
- generation instructions;
- image-generation prompts;
- process retrospectives and provenance receipts;
- private repository notes, transcripts, credentials, or session identifiers.

The local process packet may be used to audit provenance. It must not be attached to the product notebook unless a later, explicit decision creates a separate process notebook.
