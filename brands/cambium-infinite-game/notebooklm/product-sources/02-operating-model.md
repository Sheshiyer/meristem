# Cambium — Operating Model

## The viability cascade

Cambium organizes each decision cycle as four moves:

1. **Observe.** Read the current field: purpose, obligations, cash, capacity, consent, market signal, and unresolved risk.
2. **Bound.** Define the conditions that must remain true, the downside that can be absorbed, the authority available, and the action that must remain held.
3. **Act.** Choose the smallest useful move inside those boundaries.
4. **Learn.** Record the evidence, authorization, action, and outcome together so the next cycle starts from a replayable record.

The cascade favors reversible learning over unbounded motion. A green signal authorizes only the stated step.

## The wake loop

The operator is event-driven. Between events it is dormant, which is different from being finished. When an event arrives, one cycle should:

- ingest the event and update the affected world state;
- classify the event by scope and consequence;
- choose or propose the corresponding finite action;
- recompute viability margins before and after action;
- carry verified learning into the next cycle;
- persist a replay record, then return to dormancy.

This creates a continuing game without requiring continuous computation.

## Three control horizons

Cambium routes decisions across three horizons:

| Horizon | Concern | Typical action | Authority posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | Tactical correction | Reversible adjustment within an approved goal | Fast and bounded |
| Meso | Human and market interpretation | Compare intent, behavior, objections, and real signal | Propose and review |
| Macro | Vision, mission, and goals | Change purpose-bearing direction or move a setpoint | Slow, deliberate, fail-closed |

The overlaps matter. A tactical action can reveal a human reality; a human signal can reframe a goal; a rare, high-leverage event can touch all three horizons. High-consequence meaning or survival questions deserve deeper human review.

## Viability, not convergence

A finite task can converge to a result and stop. The venture above it needs a different objective: remain inside the set of states from which meaningful continuation is possible.

Cambium approximates that set through visible bounds rather than pretending to calculate it exactly. Candidate bounds include:

- solvency and cash obligations;
- legal and ethical constraints;
- mission coherence;
- human capacity and burnout risk;
- consent and delegated authority;
- evidence quality and reversibility.

When a margin collapses, the system should prefer a defensive move that restores optionality over a planned move that increases exposure.

## Evidence and simulation

Simulated founder or customer perspectives can explore possibilities. They cannot authorize a setpoint change or stand in for real adoption, replies, transactions, interviews, or observed use. Simulation proposes; reality evaluates; the responsible human commits.

## The replay record

Every consequential decision should keep four things together:

- what evidence was available;
- who or what had authority;
- what action actually occurred;
- what outcome followed.

Replay is not storytelling after the fact. It is the product's accountability spine and the basis for the next move.
