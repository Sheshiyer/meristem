# Cambium — Product Brief and Proof Status

## Product brief

Cambium is conceived as an operator above a set of bounded capabilities. It maintains a typed world state for a venture, wakes on meaningful events, routes decisions by scope, checks viability, applies or proposes a bounded action, records the result, and returns to dormancy.

Its intended components are:

- a world-state model for ventures, goals, artifacts, obligations, actors, and evidence;
- an event-driven wake loop;
- a router for micro, meso, and macro decisions;
- a viability monitor for visible operational bounds;
- a vision-to-mission-to-goal cascade controller;
- exploratory founder and customer perspectives that may propose but not commit;
- fail-closed gates for paid, external, irreversible, or purpose-changing actions;
- a deterministic replay log.

## Packaging hypothesis

The open layer gives stewards tools for bounded creation and operation. The paid layer is the continuing aesthetic and decision memory that learns the character of a venture over time. A portfolio tier coordinates multiple ventures while keeping each tenant's evidence, purpose, and learned identity separate.

This is a packaging hypothesis, not a price sheet or a claim that commercial plans are live.

## Proof status

### Defined

- enduring vision and infinite-game doctrine;
- finite-versus-infinite layer model;
- viability cascade and wake-loop behavior;
- authority and evidence principles;
- category, positioning, voice, and visual system;
- a sourcebook of conceptual visual assets.

### Requires implementation and acceptance evidence

- the long-lived operator process;
- durable world-state storage and deterministic replay across real events;
- production-grade viability monitors;
- external action adapters and their fail-closed gates;
- real-user evidence for setpoint changes;
- billing, public availability, and service-level commitments;
- measured customer or business outcomes.

## Safety and authority boundaries

Cambium must not spend, publish, contact a person, alter provider configuration, expose credentials, migrate a session, or make another consequential external change without the exact authority required for that action. One approval does not silently authorize adjacent release, deployment, payment, or account changes.

If evidence is incomplete, the product should present the uncertainty, hold the action, and preserve a reviewable path forward.

## Evaluation questions

1. Can a steward identify the venture's real viability bounds?
2. Does each consequential proposal state its evidence and authority?
3. Can the full decision be replayed after the outcome?
4. Does the system distinguish a finite instrument from the enduring purpose?
5. Does it preserve optionality when confidence or evidence is low?
6. Are conceptual, local, live, and human-accepted states clearly separated?
