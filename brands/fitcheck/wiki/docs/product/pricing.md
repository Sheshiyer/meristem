# Pricing

Fitcheck's approved commercial offer is:

| Subscription | Price |
| --- | ---: |
| Monthly | **$99 USD** |
| Yearly | **$799 USD** |

These are the only prices that current Fitcheck brand, agency, campaign, wiki,
and downstream-organ work may use. Retired pricing must not be reintroduced.

## Live Shopify listing reconciliation

On 2026-08-10, a Firecrawl fetch of the public
[Fitcheck Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/fitcheck-try-on) displayed
**Free**. That external display conflicts with the approved source of truth
above. Treat the listing as a reconciliation item until it is independently
re-fetched and shows aligned pricing.

The discrepancy does not authorize substituting the live-listing price into this
wiki or into any downstream material. It also does not establish anything about
current merchant subscriptions, installs, or revenue.

## Publishing check

Before publishing a public page or sending a campaign that names price, confirm
the final storefront and Shopify App Store displays are aligned with this page.
If they are not aligned, pause the public pricing statement and correct the
external listing rather than inventing an explanation.
