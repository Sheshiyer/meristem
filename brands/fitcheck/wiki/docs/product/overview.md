# Fitcheck

Fitcheck is **AI Fit Confidence for Shopify**: an AI try-on experience for
Shopify fashion product pages. It gives a shopper a more personal visual way to
explore a garment while they are considering it.

The intended product role is a product-page confidence layer. It is not a sizing
engine, a guarantee that a garment will fit, or a promise of a commercial result.
The shopper's judgment, product information, and the merchant's fit guidance
remain essential.

## Who it is for

The current working audience is emerging and mid-market Shopify fashion brands,
especially founder-led or operator-led teams that want to test a more personal
product-page interaction without committing to a custom commerce project. This
is a positioning hypothesis to validate with merchant interviews; it is not a
statement about current customers.

## Recommended rollout model

Start with a focused product set. Review the experience against the merchant's
brand standard, then use that merchant's own storefront evidence to decide
whether to expand. This keeps the rollout testable and avoids a generic outcome
promise.

## Product boundaries

Fitcheck may support a more informed visual decision. It must not be described
as establishing size, guaranteeing fit, reducing returns, lifting conversion, or
producing a particular revenue outcome without dated Fitcheck-specific evidence.
See [claims and evidence boundaries](claims.md).

## Commercial source of truth

The approved offer is **$99 monthly** or **$799 yearly**. See
