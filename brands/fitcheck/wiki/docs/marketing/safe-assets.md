# Safe marketing assets

This page gives downstream writers and agencies a compact, claim-safe starting
point. It does not replace the [evidence protocol](../operations/evidence-protocol.md)
or product review.

| Use | Copy |
| --- | --- |

| Decision framing | Help shoppers make a more informed visual decision before purchase. |

## Available brand assets

Use only the tracked Fitcheck assets listed in [asset origins](../../../assets/ASSET-ORIGINS.md):
the primary logo, mark, landing hero, and App Store social asset. Their
presence establishes provenance inside this project, not legal clearance or
permission to recreate third-party material.

The existing visual direction is orange, navy, and tech blue. Keep the visual
system editorial, considered, and fashion-aware. Do not use generated imagery
as proof that the product generates a particular result or works for a named
merchant.

## Copy that needs proof

Do not publish statements about conversion, revenue, returns, add-to-cart,
render speed, launch speed, sizing accuracy, fit certainty, customer volume,
reviews, or merchant satisfaction without a Fitcheck-owned, dated evidence
receipt. Do not create testimonials, customer stories, or logos from internal
campaign data.

For outreach-specific assets and personalisation, read [campaign learning](../operations/campaign-learning.md)
and the [LaCleo pilot handoff](../operations/lacleo-pilot.md). For all public
claims, start with the [evidence protocol](../operations/evidence-protocol.md).
