# Evidence protocol

This is the operating rule for every Fitcheck wiki page, campaign, agency
brief, and product message: use a source only for the precise claim it
supports. The source of record is the [Fitcheck evidence ledger](../../../research/EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md).

## Evidence classes

| Class | What it can support | What it cannot support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public source | A dated category, platform, or competitor observation. | A Fitcheck outcome, market-size conclusion, or customer result. |
| First-party product source | A verified product presence, approved offer, or recorded asset. | An unmeasured performance, privacy, or merchant-result claim. |
| Internal operating observation | A directional learning signal used to plan the next test. | Social proof, product-market fit, revenue, or campaign-performance promise. |
| Merchant proof | A specific claim only when a dated Fitcheck-owned measurement and permission record exist. | A transferable result for every merchant. |

## Required review before publishing

1. Identify the exact sentence or visual claim.
2. Link it to a ledger item or a new dated receipt.
3. Preserve the source's limitation in the surrounding copy.
4. Label internal metrics as internal signals.
5. Escalate any privacy, security, retention, fit-accuracy, conversion, or
   return-rate wording for product and legal review.

The imported Wave 1 materials are useful strategy inputs, but they are not a
substitute for fresh customer research. See the [foundation import receipt](../../../research/FOUNDATION-IMPORT.md)
for that boundary.

## Claim decisions

**Allowed now**

- "AI Fit Confidence for Shopify."
- "AI try-on for Shopify fashion product pages."
- "See the fit first."
- "$99 monthly or $799 yearly."
- A clear statement that visual try-on helps form a more informed visual
  decision and does not guarantee fit or size.

**Proof required**

- Any conversion, revenue, add-to-cart, return-rate, satisfaction, adoption,
  render-speed, or setup-time outcome.
- A claim about shopper-photo, customer-data, privacy, security, or retention
  behaviour.
- Testimonials, case studies, merchant logos, or named merchant outcomes.

**Never infer**

- Category app count as product demand.
- A competitor listing or review count as competitor installs or revenue.
- A provider-labelled lead as a qualified buyer.

## Research refresh cadence

Re-check live Shopify App Store facts immediately before a public campaign or
listing update. The current live-listing discrepancy is documented in the

public listing fetched on 2026-08-10 showed legacy pricing and unsupported
performance wording. Until it is independently re-fetched and reconciled,
do not repeat that copy anywhere downstream.
