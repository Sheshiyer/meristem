# LaCleo outbound pilot

The objective is not broad outbound scale. It is to determine whether a
tightly defined merchant segment, message, and operating cadence produces
credible conversations for Fitcheck.

## Product context for the agency

- Product: Fitcheck, an AI try-on experience for Shopify fashion product pages.
- Position: **AI Fit Confidence for Shopify**.
- Brand line: **See the fit first.**
- Commercial source of truth: **$99 monthly / $799 yearly**.
- Parent/studio context: [Thoughtseed](https://thoughtseed.space).
- Product site: [getfitcheck.space](https://getfitcheck.space).
- App distribution: [Fitcheck on Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/fitcheck-try-on).

The app listing must be re-checked before sending any public-facing link or
copy. Its 2026-08-10 fetch contained a pricing and legacy-claims discrepancy;
see the [evidence protocol](evidence-protocol.md#research-refresh-cadence).

## Initial pilot request

1. Produce a source-linked, manually verified prospect sample with role,
   company, work email, LinkedIn URL where available, and relevant account
   context.
2. Recommend a small set of testable segments. Start from Fashion
   Marketplaces, Retail Tech Teams, and Shopify Fashion Brands as hypotheses,
   not confirmed ICPs.
3. Draft short, personalised, policy-compliant email angles with one meeting
   CTA and controlled follow-up.
4. Report data quality, deliverability, positive replies, qualified
   conversations, meetings, opt-outs, and segment-level learning with the
   underlying source links.

## Approval gate before launch

Fitcheck must approve the ICP definition, geography, exclusions, contact
fields, seed list, suppression approach, copy, reply routing, cost breakdown,
and success-review cadence before sends begin. The agency must distinguish its
service fee, data cost, sending-infrastructure cost, and optional pre-sales
handling.

## Qualification and reporting

A provider label is not qualification. Define qualification jointly before the
pilot: relevant company, relevant role, a credible problem fit, expressed
interest or agreed next step, and an owner for follow-up.

Report learning rather than vanity metrics. The existing internal baseline—1,241
sends, 18 replies, five provider-labelled hot leads, and $37.23 recorded
spend—may inform the test design but must not appear as product or agency
proof. See [campaign learning](campaign-learning.md).

## Non-negotiable claims

- Do not claim conversion lift, revenue lift, return reduction, sizing
  accuracy, setup time, adoption, or customer satisfaction.
- Do not use merchant logos, testimonials, case studies, or inferred personal
  details without an attributable record and permission.
- Do not revive retired price language. The only approved subscription prices
  are $99 monthly and $799 yearly.
- Do not suggest affiliation with or reproduce the positioning of the
  same-name [Synthenova FitCheck listing](https://apps.shopify.com/fitcheck-1).

Apply the [evidence protocol](evidence-protocol.md) and use [safe marketing assets](../marketing/safe-assets.md)
when the agency requests creative or landing-page inputs.
