# Visual guidelines

Fitcheck should feel editorial, confident, and useful—not like a generic AI
widget. Existing Fitcheck identity assets are the production anchor. This page
extends their system; it does not establish legal clearance or permission to
reuse third-party imagery.

## Colour

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fit Orange | `#FF6B35` | One decisive CTA or interaction accent |
| Editorial Navy | `#1A1A2E` | Editorial hierarchy, headings, and high-confidence surfaces |
| Midnight Blue | `#16213E` | Supporting dark surfaces |
| Warm white | `#FAFAFA` | Calm neutral canvas |

Use orange with restraint: it should signal the decision or interaction, not
fill every surface. Use navy for hierarchy and white text on navy surfaces.
The inherited palette reports a 16.2:1 contrast ratio for navy/white and 4.7:1
for white on the darker orange `#D94E20`; validate final component states in
the implementation rather than treating those values as a complete accessibility
audit.

## Typography

- **DM Serif Display**: headlines and campaign display, conveying editorial
  fashion perspective.
- **Inter**: interface, body, and product information, keeping Shopify content
  readable.

Use the serif for a clear point of view, not long UI passages. Use Inter at a
minimum readable size with sufficient spacing and contrast.

## Imagery and illustration

Photography should be intimate, editorial, and product-relevant: soft
directional studio light, authentic shadow, warm neutrals, restrained Fit
Orange, and useful garment detail. Show diverse adult shoppers without turning
their bodies into proof of a fit result.

Illustration should be low-complexity geometric interface language: navy
linework, one orange focal point, rounded 2px-style icons, and no decorative
claims. Suitable icon concepts include try-on, product page, shopper confidence,
setup, and privacy.

## Guardrails

- Do not imply perfect fit through an image, before/after, or generated scene.
- Do not use AI-generated imagery as factual product proof.
- Do not imitate Synthenova's listing language or visual identity.
- Confirm asset provenance before production use; see
  [asset origins](../../../assets/ASSET-ORIGINS.md).

The visual system should reinforce the principles in
[mission and values](mission-values.md) and the language rules in
[voice and tone](voice-tone.md). It is designed for the working
[primary persona](../audience/primary-persona.md), which remains a
hypothesis pending merchant interviews.
