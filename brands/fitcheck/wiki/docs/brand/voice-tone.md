# Voice and tone

Fitcheck is **precise, fashion-aware, and warm**. It speaks as a clear-eyed
fashion-commerce guide: practical with merchants and respectful of shoppers'
own body knowledge.

## Voice principles

- Use plain, editorial language with concrete Shopify and product-page context.
- Explain what AI try-on can help a shopper explore, then state what it cannot
  establish.
- Invite a merchant to test and learn in its own storefront.
- Make privacy, accessibility, and limitations clear rather than decorative.

The desired effect is confidence through clarity—not hype, novelty, or
technical theatre.

## Language to prefer

Use: **fit confidence**, **more informed**, **product page**, **shopper**,
**merchant**, **guided setup**, **considered**, **editorial**, **test**, and
**learn**.

Avoid: **guaranteed**, **perfect fit**, **magic**, **revolutionary**,
**best-in-class**, **instant**, **conversion lift**, and **return reduction**.

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Adjustment | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product announcement | Measured excitement | “Fitcheck brings a more personal try-on moment to Shopify product pages.” |
| Education | Clear and evidence-led | “Start with a focused product set, then learn from your own storefront.” |
| Support | Patient and explicit | “Here is what Fitcheck can help a shopper explore—and what it cannot establish.” |
| Sales | Confident, never inflated | “Choose $99 monthly or $799 yearly for access to Fitcheck.” |
| Issue response | Direct and accountable | “We take this seriously and will explain the next step plainly.” |

## Claim discipline

Do not transform a category problem into a Fitcheck outcome. Fit uncertainty is
a credible problem to explore, but Fitcheck has no approved conversion, return,
revenue, sizing-accuracy, adoption, or satisfaction claim. Any shopper-photo
or customer-data wording also requires product and privacy review.
