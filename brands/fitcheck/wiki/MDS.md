# Fitcheck Messaging Direction Summary

## The one-line position

**Fitcheck is AI Fit Confidence for Shopify: an editorial, guided AI try-on
experience for fashion product pages.**

## The problem worth solving

Static product imagery can leave a shopper’s personal fit question open.
Apparel fit uncertainty is a legitimate ecommerce problem, but Fitcheck must
not claim it solves every sizing or returns question.

## The product promise

Give shoppers a more personal visual starting point on the product page, then
help merchants learn from a focused rollout in their own storefront.

## Why this is different

1. **Fit confidence, not AI novelty.** The language focuses on an informed
   visual decision, not technological spectacle.
2. **Guided, bounded learning.** A merchant can assess the experience on a
   focused product set instead of treating it as an open-ended custom project.
3. **Trust in practice.** Fitcheck states what it can help explore and what it
   cannot prove.

## Audience and distribution

The primary working ICP is the founder or ecommerce operator at an emerging to
mid-market Shopify fashion brand. It is a hypothesis to validate—not a claim
about current customers. Initial distribution is targeted outreach and guided
setup. App Store search is a useful presence, not the initial growth engine.

Existing internal outbound is a learning signal: 1,241 sends, 18 replies, five
provider-labelled hot leads, and $37.23. Fashion Marketplaces had a 3.0% reply
rate; Retail Tech Teams and Shopify Fashion Brands each supplied two hot labels.
This is prioritization input, not commercial proof.

## Voice

Precise, fashion-aware, warm. Say “more informed,” “product page,” “fit
confidence,” “guided setup,” and “merchant-specific learning.” Avoid
“guaranteed,” “perfect fit,” “conversion lift,” “return reduction,” “instant,”
and other unverified outcomes.

## Commercial rule

The brand-system source of truth is **$99 monthly / $799 yearly**. The current
live Shopify listing displays different information and is explicitly held as a
reconciliation item in the [evidence ledger](../research/EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md).

## Non-negotiable proof rules

- Demonstrate the product flow; do not substitute generated imagery for proof.
- Link privacy and data-practice claims to an audited implementation.
- Attribute merchant outcomes to that merchant’s dated measurement.
- Keep Synthenova as a category reference only; never mimic its language or
  visual identity.
