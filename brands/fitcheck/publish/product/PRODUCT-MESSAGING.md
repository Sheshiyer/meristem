# Fitcheck product messaging

## Core description

Fitcheck is AI Fit Confidence for Shopify: a visual try-on experience for
fashion product pages.

## Product promise

Give shoppers a more personal visual starting point when they are exploring a
garment on a product page.

## Key messages

### Make the product page more visual

Fitcheck adds a visual try-on moment to the fashion product page, helping a
shopper engage with a garment in a more personal way.

### Keep the experience fashion-aware

The product is framed around considered product presentation, clear language,
and a shopping moment that respects the merchant's brand.

### Start focused

Merchants can begin with a selected product set and learn from the experience
in their own storefront.

## Voice

Precise, fashion-aware, warm. Use plain product language. Show the experience
instead of making broad promises.
