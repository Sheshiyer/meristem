# Fitcheck

Fitcheck is **AI Fit Confidence for Shopify**. It brings a visual try-on
experience to fashion product pages, giving shoppers a more personal visual
starting point while they explore a garment.

For fashion merchants, Fitcheck is a product-page confidence layer: a focused
experience that can sit alongside the product information shoppers already use
to make their own decisions. It is designed for brands that want their product
page to feel more considered, more visual, and more aligned with the way people
shop fashion online.

Fitcheck does not replace a shopper's judgment, the merchant's product details,
or fit guidance. It helps make the product-page moment easier to explore.

## What shoppers see

- A visual try-on moment on a fashion product page.
- A more personal way to explore a garment before deciding whether to buy.
- Product information and merchant guidance in the context of the existing
  storefront.

## What merchants get

- A fashion-aware product-page experience.
- A focused way to introduce visual try-on to selected products.
- A product direction that can be assessed in the merchant's own storefront.
