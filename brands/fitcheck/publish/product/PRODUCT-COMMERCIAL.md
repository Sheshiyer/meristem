# Fitcheck commercial overview

Fitcheck is available as a Shopify fashion product-page experience.

| Plan | Price |
| --- | ---: |
| Monthly | **$99 USD** |
| Yearly | **$799 USD** |

The annual plan is for merchants who prefer a longer-term product-page program;
the monthly plan is for merchants who prefer a monthly subscription. Both plans
refer to the same Fitcheck product experience.

## A clear product boundary

Fitcheck is a visual try-on experience. It supports exploration on a product
page; it is not a statement that a garment will fit a particular person or that
a merchant will see a particular commercial result.
