# Fitcheck product FAQ

## What is Fitcheck?

Fitcheck is AI Fit Confidence for Shopify, a visual try-on experience for
fashion product pages.

## Who is it for?

Fitcheck is designed for Shopify fashion merchants that want to make the
product-page experience more visual and considered.

## What does it add to a product page?

It adds a visual try-on moment that gives shoppers another way to explore a
garment alongside the merchant's existing product information.

## Does Fitcheck guarantee fit?

No. A visual try-on experience does not replace a shopper's judgment, the
merchant's product information, or fit guidance.

## How much does Fitcheck cost?

Fitcheck is **$99 USD monthly** or **$799 USD yearly**.
