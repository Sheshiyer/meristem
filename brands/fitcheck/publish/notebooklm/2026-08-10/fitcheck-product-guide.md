# Fitcheck: A Product-First Guide for Shopify Fashion Merchants

## 1. Product Overview

**Definition & Purpose**
Fitcheck is AI Fit Confidence for Shopify. It is a visual try-on experience specifically built for fashion product pages, providing shoppers with a personal visual starting point to explore garments.

**The Product Promise**
Fitcheck offers shoppers a more personal way to engage with a garment on a product page. By providing a visual starting point for exploration, it creates a shopping moment focused on the individual’s interaction with the product.

**The Confidence Layer**
As a product-page confidence layer, Fitcheck serves as a focused experience that sits alongside the product information and merchant guidance shoppers already use. It is framed around considered product presentation and clear language, ensuring the experience respects the merchant’s brand aesthetic.

## 2. The Shopper Experience

**On-Page Interaction**
When interacting with Fitcheck on a product page, shoppers encounter:
*   **A visual try-on moment:** A dedicated experience on the fashion product page.
*   **Personal exploration:** A more personal way to explore a garment before deciding whether to buy.
*   **Contextual information:** Access to product details and merchant guidance within the existing storefront environment.

**Integration with Merchant Data**
The Fitcheck experience is designed to sit directly alongside the merchant’s existing product information. It functions within the context of the storefront, appearing where shoppers are already looking for product details and guidance.

**Decision Support**
Fitcheck facilitates exploration by making the product-page moment easier to explore. This visual support does not replace a shopper's judgment, the merchant's specific product details, or provided fit guidance.

## 3. Merchant Suitability

**Target Audience**
Fitcheck is designed for Shopify fashion brands that want to make their product-page experience more visual and considered. It is ideal for merchants looking to align their storefronts with modern online shopping behaviors through a more personal digital experience.

**Merchant Benefits**
Fitcheck provides a fashion-aware product-page experience. It offers merchants a focused way to introduce visual try-on technology to a selection of products while maintaining a professional and considered presentation.

## 4. Implementation Path

**Getting Started**
Merchants can begin using Fitcheck by focusing on a selected product set. This allows for a focused introduction of the visual try-on experience to the storefront.

**Storefront Assessment**
The product direction is designed to be assessed directly within the merchant's own storefront. This allows merchants to learn from the experience in their own environment.

## 6. Product FAQ

**What is Fitcheck?**
Fitcheck is AI Fit Confidence for Shopify, a visual try-on experience for fashion product pages.

**Who is it for?**
It is designed for Shopify fashion merchants who want to make their product-page experience more visual and considered.

**Does Fitcheck guarantee fit?**
No. A visual try-on experience does not replace a shopper's judgment, the merchant's product information, or fit guidance. It is not a statement that a garment will fit a particular person.

**What does it add to a product page?**
It adds a visual try-on moment that gives shoppers another way to explore a garment alongside the merchant's existing product information.
