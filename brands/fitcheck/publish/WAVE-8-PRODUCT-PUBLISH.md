pricing, and FAQ only.

## Product language

Fitcheck is **AI Fit Confidence for Shopify**: a visual try-on experience for
fashion product pages. It gives shoppers a more personal visual starting point
while they explore a garment.

The current offer is **$99 USD monthly** or **$799 USD yearly**.

## Publishing boundary

This is a publication-ready content package, not an external deployment. Before
a public site or App Store page changes, confirm the live commercial display and
the implemented product behavior.
