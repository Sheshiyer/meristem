# Fitcheck brand brief

Fitcheck remains the brand name. Its public descriptor is **AI Fit Confidence
for Shopify**. The product is positioned as a product-page confidence layer for
Shopify fashion merchants—not as a promise of conversion lift or return
reduction.

The immediate growth motion is distribution-led: targeted merchant outreach,
guided setup, and merchant-specific measurement. Organic App Store discovery
is useful but not the initial acquisition dependency.

## Commercial source of truth

Use **$99 monthly** and **$799 yearly** in all current Fitcheck materials.

## Evidence and claim rules

The source ledger at [research/EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md](research/EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md)
separates public-source facts, internal campaign observations, hypotheses, and
claims that must not be made without proof. Treat it as the input authority for
Wave 1.
