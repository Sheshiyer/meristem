# Fitcheck NotebookLM source pack

This is the bounded source set for a B2B brand-system notebook. It supports
internal wiki and agency work; it is not a public source of product claims.

## Include

- `../BRAND-BRIEF.md` — approved brand framing and commercial source of truth.
- `EVIDENCE-LEDGER.md` — dated research, source links, conflicts, and campaign
  signals.
- `../wiki/MDS.md` — messaging direction summary for the internal wiki.
- `../wiki/DOWNSTREAM-HANDOFF.md` — downstream operating constraints.
- `../../../../fitcheck-landing/.project/HANDOFF.md` — product-repository
  commercial checkpoint (extract only the approved `$99` monthly / `$799`
  yearly decision; do not use its unrelated packet history as copy).

## Exclude

- `legacy-unvalidated/` — provenance only; never upload to the notebook.
- New photos, lifestyle imagery, or broad social-content material.
- Any customer, founder, credential, or private-vault material.

## Allowed generated artifacts

1. A briefing report that separates facts, observed campaign signals, source
   conflicts, hypotheses, and open validation questions.
2. A mind map of the brand system and wiki information architecture.
3. A data table mapping claims to source, confidence, owner, and next check.

No image, video, audio, or slide-deck generation is required for this B2B phase.
Existing approved logo, product, and Shopify assets remain the visual system.

The logged-in CLI created `Fitcheck — Brand System · Internal`, added the four
sources above, and generated the three allowed internal artifacts. Their exact
notebook/source/artifact IDs and local checksums are recorded in
`notebooklm/2026-08-10/RECEIPT.md`. The artifacts were generated before the
pipeline reached Wave 7, then reconciled as Wave 7 internal synthesis inputs
