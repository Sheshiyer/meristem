# Fitcheck Internal Brand-System Briefing (Phase 1)

## 1. Brand Essence and Canonical Positioning

**One-Line Position**
Fitcheck is AI Fit Confidence for Shopify: an editorial, guided AI try-on experience for fashion product pages.

> **Public Descriptor: AI Fit Confidence for Shopify**

**The Problem Worth Solving and Product Promise**
Static product imagery often leaves a shopper’s personal fit questions unanswered. Apparel fit uncertainty is a legitimate e-commerce challenge that impacts shopper confidence. The Fitcheck product promise is to provide shoppers with a more personal visual starting point directly on the product page, allowing merchants to learn through a focused rollout within their own storefronts. Consistent with our evidence-led stance, Fitcheck makes no claims regarding guaranteed conversion lift or return reduction.

**Why This Is Different**
*   **Fit Confidence, Not AI Novelty:** Messaging focuses on enabling informed visual decisions rather than emphasizing technological spectacle.
*   **Guided, Bounded Learning:** Merchants can assess the experience on a specific, focused product set rather than managing an open-ended, high-risk custom project.
*   **Trust in Practice:** The brand maintains transparency regarding the capabilities of the technology, explicitly stating what it can help explore and what it cannot prove.

## 3. Governance: Explicit Claim Guardrails

### Prohibited Claims Checklist
The following claims are currently blocked from all internal and external communication:
*   **Conversion/Revenue Lift:** Requires merchant-specific, dated measurement; currently unverified.
*   **Return Reduction:** Requires merchant-specific, dated measurement; currently unverified.
*   **Perfect Fit / Guaranteed Results:** A try-on result cannot guarantee size, appearance, or purchase suitability; blocked to prevent misleading shoppers.
*   **Instant Results:** Outcomes are unverified and must not be characterized as "instant" or "immediate."
*   **User Volume or Quality:** Blocked due to a lack of Fitcheck-specific source data or audited user metrics.
*   **Setup Speed:** Claims of "48-hour launch" or similar are blocked; requires a Fitcheck-specific source and verification of current operational capacity.

### Voice and Terminology
**Tone Directive:** Precise, fashion-aware, and warm.

| Use These Terms | Avoid These Terms |
| :--- | :--- |
| More informed | Guaranteed |

| Merchant-specific learning | Instant / Unverified outcomes |

### Non-Negotiable Proof Rules
*   **Demonstrate Flow:** Show the actual product flow; do not use generated imagery as a substitute for proof.
*   **Audit Privacy:** Link all privacy and data-practice claims to an audited implementation.
*   **Attribute Outcomes:** Attribute any merchant outcomes only to that merchant’s specific, dated measurement.
*   **Competitive Separation:** Maintain Synthenova as a category reference only; never mimic their language or visual identity.

## 4. Market Context & Ecosystem Facts

### Shopify Environment
*   **Category Density:** The virtual-try-on category on Shopify is saturated, with approximately 297 apps as of August 2026 [ID P1]. Discoverability requires a deliberate distribution plan rather than reliance on organic search.
*   **Merchant Requirements:** Shopify mandates that apps be secure, truthful, and privacy-safe, requiring accurate sync and merchant access to collected data [ID P3].
*   **Privacy & Compliance:** Apps must respond to compliance-webhook privacy requests and adhere to strict data minimization for protected customer data [ID P4].

### Competitive Flag: Synthenova [ID P2]
A name collision exists with Synthenova’s "FitCheck" listing. To maintain clear market differentiation, Fitcheck must:
*   Strictly use the descriptor "**AI Fit Confidence for Shopify**."
*   Apply the $99/$799 pricing model (contrasting Synthenova’s free/usage-based model).
*   Maintain a distinct, premium visual identity that avoids Synthenova’s language.

### Technical Foundations
*   **Data Flows:** Any shopper-photo or customer-data flow requires a specific privacy and retention review [ID P4].
*   **Platform Fact:** Fit uncertainty is a credible merchant problem; apparel and footwear commonly have above-average return rates [ID P5].
