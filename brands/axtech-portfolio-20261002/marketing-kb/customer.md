## Segment discipline

- Electrical-only, plumbing-only and CVC-only are separate; mixed plumbing+CVC requires evidence of both activities.
- Architects, BET electricity, BET fluids and BET construction/structure retain distinct roles; relevant offer fit must be documented.
- Use SIREN for business deduplication, SIRET for establishment; name/NAF alone does not prove the actual or exclusive trade.
- One company may have multiple verified activities; choose one relevant communication path, preserving activity evidence and uncertainty.
- Axtech routes; Ecoled/HeyZack have relevant researched electrical/MEP hypotheses. Kartezzi requires architecture/interior-project fit.
- Wave targets mobile retail only in a separate future scope; Symphonics stays held. Consumers/Airbnb owners are outside this task.
- France region/department helps routing; unknown coverage remains unknown. All sizes are eligible, not a claim of exhaustive leads.
