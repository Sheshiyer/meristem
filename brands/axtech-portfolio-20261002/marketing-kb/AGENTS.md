# Marketing KB operating rules

## Purpose and authority

This pack supports source-bounded draft preparation for Axtech's portfolio. It does not run providers or mutate live tools.
Priority: current user instructions → current brand dossier/evidence → shared KB links/rules → campaign brief/decisions → examples.
Read [.agents/product-marketing-context.md](.agents/product-marketing-context.md) for initial orientation; explicit local routing only.
The six root files link to each brand authority. A claim/offer change belongs in that authority first; do not create function-specific offers.
Research supplies attributed first-party claims, not verified performance. A competitor's capabilities never become ours.
No API keys, personal lead records, provider prompts, client binaries or live credentials belong here.

## Scope and conflicts

Current task: B2B, France, all business sizes including autoentrepreneurs; trade evidence controls segment membership.
Separate electricity, plumbing, CVC, verified plumbing+CVC, architects, BET electricity/fluids and BET construction/structure.
Wave remains outside MEP; Symphonics has no known offer and is held. Kartezzi uses architecture/interior-design routing only where relevant.
France is the prospect market. Region/department records do not imply verified national installation or delivery coverage.
Group membership, CHR metadata, legal seat, Kartezzi history/delays and technical claims retain the conflicts in the linked dossiers.
Flag a conflict with its source, consequence and missing evidence; do not silently reconcile or invent an answer.

Brandmint wave execution must use the existing shell runner; this KB does not bypass it or claim wave completion.

## Draft review and live gates

Save drafts under [production](campaigns/b2b-mep-fr-preparation/production/README.md), labeled internal, with brand, trade, source IDs and unresolved claims.
Illustrative examples accepted for internal drafting are agent-curated and have no human approval or performance evidence.
No sends, posts, calls, Explee project/campaign/budget changes or marketplace writes are authorized by this pack.
Pricing, new proof, legal/technical promises and external publication require the current owner decision and the applicable verified evidence.
Custom ERP/MCP, sender identity, suppression and synthetic end-to-end transfer must be proven before live activation is proposed.
A future approval must record approver, date, exact artifact and permitted action; a draft review is not send authority.

## Learning after an actual review

Follow the [LearnLoop skill workflow](/Users/sheshnarayaniyer/.agents/skills/MarketingAgentKnowledgeBase/Workflows/LearnLoop.md).
Record edits and rationale in campaign decisions; label reviewer/source/date, then update an example or rule only within authorized scope.
Never mark illustrative drafts human-approved, infer consent from interest, or fabricate analytics.
