# Email preparation playbook

Read [voice](../voice.md), [offer](../offer.md), [proof](../proof.md) and the selected brand's source pair before drafting.
Recurring task: one French first-contact draft for one evidenced trade/offer hypothesis, with no recipient data or send operation.

1. Select one brand and exclusive trade; verify both activities for a mixed segment.
2. Note dossier/evidence source IDs and the qualifying need; reject an unsupported offer fit.
3. Draft a clear subject, vous opening, one relevant topic and one low-pressure question.
4. Put unverified sender and regional fields in internal metadata, not fabricated personalisation.
5. Annotate unresolved compatibility/conditions; keep commercial promises out of the body.
6. Check [email checks](checks.md), then save in campaign production with internal status.

Examples: [accepted for internal draft](examples/accepted-for-internal-draft.md), [rejected](examples/rejected.md).
Do not create follow-up schedules, enroll leads, reply to leads or change Explee campaigns from this pack.
A possible later follow-up must be reviewed for relevance, prior response and suppression before drafting or sending.
Missing inputs are research tasks; no automatic substitute company, offer, region or contact identity.
