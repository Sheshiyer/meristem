# Content return checks

- Internal draft label, brand, role, locale and source IDs present.
- One professional topic, plain French vous, no invented project/client narrative.
- No unsupported performance, saving, certification, stock, price or region promise.
- Competitor/factory photos and logos are neither our references nor approved assets.
- A checklist is framed as general preparation, not a compliance certificate or completed technical study.
- Electrical/BET, fluids/CVC and architecture/structure roles retain their differences.
- Kartezzi interior-design content does not become MEP; Wave MEP and Symphonics generation are excluded.
- No fake owner approval, A/B result, impression, conversion or engagement metric.
- Visual reference has an existing source path and known rights gap; no generated asset claimed.
- Any CTA needs a future verified destination/sender and capacity review before public use.
- Platform is unpublished; no tool write or scheduled post is requested by this pack.
