# Content preparation playbook

Read shared [voice](../voice.md), [offer](../offer.md), selected brand research and platform reference; do not load every channel.
Recurring task: one source-linked French educational post about a professional qualification question.

1. Choose brand, role and one useful work topic supported by the dossier.
2. Identify a practical question/checklist; label general guidance or illustrative scenarios.
3. Draft one clear idea and optional conversation prompt, without commercial proof or invented customer context.
4. Include source IDs and pending facts in internal notes; product claims require the actual approval record.
5. Apply [content checks](checks.md), save draft and keep the platform unpublished.
