## Proof record before public use

Record: statement/metric and definition, measured period (or not applicable), primary source, date/version, approval owner/date and permitted channel.
Current records: all commercial claims lack public approval in these ledgers; measurement periods for commercial performance are unavailable.
AX TECH's active legal identity is independently sourced; it does not prove delivery, savings, service area or other brand affiliation.
Exa/Firecrawl receipts prove retrieved text and source attribution, not the truth of every marketing claim.
Perplexity HTTP200 registration responses are semantic authentication failures. Symphonics Firecrawl content is empty/unusable.
Do not promote counters, synthetic metrics, public website clients, competitor certifications or photos into our proof.

Open gaps: owner-approved case studies, primary technical reports, image/logo permissions and real analytics exports with defined periods.
