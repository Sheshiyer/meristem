## Rules grounded in annotated examples

- Use a question about a real work topic; see [accepted email](email/examples/accepted-for-internal-draft.md), not invented personalisation.
- Replace quantified savings and universal packages with qualification; see [rejected email](email/examples/rejected.md).
- Name the relevant brand/trade; see [accepted content](content/examples/accepted-for-internal-draft.md), not a mixed portfolio catalogue.
- Avoid « leader », « garanti », « partout en France », automatic CEE and unsupported client logos; see [rejected content](content/examples/rejected.md).
- A draft signature uses a visible placeholder until sender identity is verified; internal placeholders must never be sent.
- Keep privacy/objection handling clear in eventual send-ready work; transport and legal requirements remain to be verified before activation.

No example is human-approved or proven to perform. Record an actual owner edit before treating it as brand voice evidence.
